Text section third-party components

Runtime/Text/ollama is a copy of Ollama 0.33.3 for Windows (https://github.com/ollama/ollama, MIT License) together with the
llama.cpp, GGML and other libraries it bundles; their license texts are in Runtime/Text/ollama/lib/ollama/*LICENSE* and *COPYING*.
The AMD ROCm libraries are not installed. The setup program downloads this copy from the official GitHub release and checks its SHA-256.

Models/Text/ollama is the private model store of the studio. It holds the models you chose in the setup or in the Components tab
(from the public Ollama registry, or copied from your own Ollama). Each model comes with its own license, shown by Ollama
("ollama show <model> --license") and stored in the model's manifest; the Gemma family is covered by the Gemma terms of use.

The studio runs its own Ollama process on 127.0.0.1 (own port, own model folder, no cloud features) and does not use, start, stop or
change an Ollama installation of the user.
